About the District

A City of Heritage & Nature

Sambalpur, located on the banks of the sacred Mahanadi river, is one of the most culturally rich districts of Odisha. Home to the legendary Hirakud Dam — one of the world's longest earthen dams — it seamlessly blends natural grandeur with centuries of history.

The district is celebrated globally for its Sambalpuri saree and Bomkai weave, revered for the Samaleswari temple, and admired for its vibrant folk traditions including the exuberant Sambalpuri dance.

Places to Visit

Top 10 Destinations

01
Hirakud Dam
Engineering Marvel

World's longest earthen dam; stunning sunsets & boating on the Mahanadi reservoir.

02
Samaleswari Temple
Sacred Shrine

The presiding deity of Sambalpur, Goddess Samalei, revered by millions across Odisha.

03
Debrigarh Wildlife Sanctuary
Nature Reserve

Dense forest teeming with leopards, hyenas, and over 200 bird species around Hirakud.

04
Huma Temple
Leaning Temple

The only "leaning temple" of India, dedicated to Lord Shiva and perched on the Mahanadi bank.

05
Ushakothi Wildlife Sanctuary
Tiger Reserve

Haunt of tigers and elephants; pristine sal forests draped in mist and birdsong.

06
Gandhamardan Hills
Pilgrimage & Trek

Mythologically famous hills believed to hold the medicinal herbs used by Hanuman.

07
Badrama Wildlife Sanctuary
Eco Tourism

A quiet gem for birdwatchers and nature lovers exploring Western Odisha's forests.

08
Sambalpur City Market
Culture & Shopping

Vibrant bazaars selling Sambalpuri sarees, Bomkai silk, and traditional silver jewellery.

09
Nrusinghanath Temple
Ancient Temple

A 14th-century temple nestled in the Gandhamardan foothills, devoted to Lord Nrusingha.

10
Bargarh Weaving Villages
Craft Heritage

Watch master weavers create world-renowned Ikat textiles in centuries-old handloom workshops.

Local Delicacies

Flavours of Sambalpur

🍚
Pakhala
Fermented rice soaked in water, served with fried fish, roasted papad, and saga bhaja — a staple of Odia life.
🐟
Mahanadi Rohu Curry
Fresh rohu from the Mahanadi cooked in mustard gravy with aromatic local spices.
🍬
Chhena Poda
Odisha's signature burnt cheesecake — caramelized cottage cheese baked to perfection with cardamom.
🫙
Badi Chura
Crumbled sun-dried lentil dumplings mixed with onion, green chilli, and mustard oil — a beloved snack.
🌿
Maandiya
Ragi (finger millet) porridge, a traditional health drink of the tribal heartland, lightly sweetened.
🍮
Arisa Pitha
Rice flour pancakes sweetened with jaggery and deep fried — a festive favourite during Nuakhai.
🥘
Dalma
Lentils slow-cooked with seasonal vegetables, tempered with cumin and dried chillis — soul food of Odisha.
🌶️
Chakta
A tangy raw papaya & raw mango salad with mustard and chilli — sharp, refreshing, uniquely Sambalpuri.

Celebrations

Festivals of Sambalpur

September
Nuakhai Juhar
Odisha's most beloved harvest festival where new rice is first offered to Goddess Samaleswari before being consumed by the family.
January
Sambalpur Sambalpuri Mahotsav
A vibrant cultural extravaganza showcasing folk dances like Sambalpuri, Dalkhai, Rasarkeli, and Karma nacha.
March – April
Sitalsasthi
The grand marriage procession of Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati — celebrated with elaborate tableaux across the city streets.
October
Durga Puja
Elaborate pandals and processions fill Sambalpur with colour; the city's Devi Mandaps are famous throughout Western Odisha.
November
Diwali & Kali Puja
A festival of lights along the Mahanadi's banks; tribal communities celebrate with fire rituals and folk music.
January
Pous Sankranti
Rice-harvest thanksgiving with Pitha-making competitions, folk theatre, and worship at household shrines at dawn.

Traditional Crafts

Arts & Crafts

🧵
Sambalpuri Saree
World-famous Ikat-dyed handloom saree where threads are tie-dyed before weaving to create geometric patterns.
🥻
Bomkai Silk
Luxurious extra-weft silk with tribal motifs — a GI-tagged textile treasured by connoisseurs worldwide.
💃
Sambalpuri Dance
A spirited folk dance tradition with energetic footwork, vibrant costumes, and devotional themes.
🥁
Dalkhai Folk Music
Percussion-driven folk songs sung by women during harvest festivals — raw, rhythmic, and mesmerising.
🪘
Karma Dance
Tribal ritual dance performed around the Karma tree to honour the deity of fate and prosperity.
🖼️
Tribal Jewellery & Dokra
Ancient lost-wax cast metalwork and silver tribal jewellery unique to the artisans of Western Odisha.

Travel Guide

How to Reach Sambalpur

✈️
By Air
The nearest airport is Veer Surendra Sai Airport, Jharsuguda (JRG), just 55 km away. Connected to Kolkata, Hyderabad, and Raipur. Bhubaneswar (BBI) is 360 km — the largest hub in Odisha.
🚆
By Train
Sambalpur Railway Station and Sambalpur Road Station are well-connected. Major trains like Ispat Express (Bhubaneswar–Mumbai), Hirakhand Express, and Shatabdi connect it to Bhubaneswar, Kolkata, and Delhi.
🚗
By Road
Located on NH-55 (Kolkata–Nagpur Highway). State buses and private vehicles connect Sambalpur to Bhubaneswar (320 km), Raipur (260 km), and Ranchi (340 km). Roads are scenic through Odisha's forests.
